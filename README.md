> *"Friends don't lie."* – Eleven

## Overview

**Stranger Things** is a popular Netflix original sci-fi horror series created by the Duffer Brothers. Set in the 1980s, it blends supernatural elements with government conspiracy, friendship, nostalgia, and creepy creatures from another dimension.

---

## 🧠 Key Themes

- **The Upside Down** – A parallel, sinister dimension.
- **Government Experiments** – Including telekinesis and mind control.
- **Friendship & Loyalty** – The heart of the show.
- **1980s Nostalgia** – Music, fashion, tech, and pop culture references.

---
